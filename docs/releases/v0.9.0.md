## English

- Drag the handle or account name in **Manage Accounts** to reorder accounts. The quota cards follow the same order, which is saved across restarts.
- Click anywhere on the **Manage Accounts** heading to expand or collapse it.
- Display order is independent of automatic request scheduling and duplicate-account selection.
- Developer ID signed and Apple-notarized, with tickets stapled to the app and DMG.

**Requirements:** Apple Silicon (arm64), macOS 14 or later, and a separately installed [Codex CLI](https://github.com/openai/codex#installing-and-running-codex-cli).

**Install:** Quit the existing app, open `Codex-Pulse-0.9.0.dmg`, and drag **Codex Pulse** into **Applications**. Existing accounts and scheduling checkpoints are retained.

## 简体中文

- 在**管理账号**中拖动手柄或账号名称调整顺序；额度卡片按同一顺序展示，重启后保留。
- 点击**管理账号**标题整行即可展开或收起。
- 展示排序独立于自动请求调度，不改变重复账号的自动请求选择。
- 使用 Developer ID 签名，应用与 DMG 均通过 Apple 公证并附加凭证。

**要求：** Apple Silicon（arm64）、macOS 14 及以上，另需安装 [Codex CLI](https://github.com/openai/codex#installing-and-running-codex-cli)。

**安装：** 先退出旧版，打开 `Codex-Pulse-0.9.0.dmg`，将 **Codex Pulse** 拖入 **Applications**。保留已有账号和调度检查点。
