## English

- **Refresh Now** can retry an incomplete or unconfirmed request once per five-hour window, after checking the latest quota. Successful requests are not retried.
- The retry allowance is saved before sending, so repeated clicks and restarts cannot trigger more retries in that window. Background scheduling keeps its existing safeguards.
- A timed-out request may already have succeeded; a retry can consume additional quota. This release adds controlled retrying, rather than fixing the underlying timeout.
- Developer ID signed and Apple-notarized, with tickets stapled to the app and DMG.

**Requirements:** Apple Silicon (arm64), macOS 14 or later, and a separately installed [Codex CLI](https://github.com/openai/codex#installing-and-running-codex-cli).

**Install:** Quit the existing app, open `Codex-Pulse-0.9.1.dmg`, and drag **Codex Pulse** into **Applications**. Existing accounts and scheduling checkpoints are retained.

## 简体中文

- 点击**立即刷新**，查询最新额度后，可对失败或结果未确认的请求每个五小时窗口补试一次；成功请求不补试。
- 补试预算在请求前保存，连续点击或重启不会再次补试；后台调度保留原有防重复规则。
- 超时请求可能实际已成功，补试可能额外消耗额度。本版提供限次补试，未修复原超时根因。
- 使用 Developer ID 签名，应用与 DMG 均通过 Apple 公证并附加凭证。

**要求：** Apple Silicon（arm64）、macOS 14 及以上，另需安装 [Codex CLI](https://github.com/openai/codex#installing-and-running-codex-cli)。

**安装：** 先退出旧版，打开 `Codex-Pulse-0.9.1.dmg`，将 **Codex Pulse** 拖入 **Applications**。保留已有账号和调度检查点。
